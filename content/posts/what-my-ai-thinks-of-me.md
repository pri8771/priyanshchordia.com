*It read four years of my chats. I had a list of 352 other possibilities.*

I had my AI assistants read four years of my own chats and commit history and write a profile of me. In its findings file, every finding comes with its evidence, a confidence level and a line that starts "Could be wrong if". The third revision landed on November 21.

I can build things, so there was quite a bit to read. About 70 app designs since April, by its count. Then it listed who uses them.

![Agent Bus and the gateway have only him as a user](/blog-media/what-my-ai-thinks-of-me/card-1.png)

I use both. It would be nice to count that as two.

Then it looked for evidence that I talk to customers. In June I had a landing page, a CRM and a list of 352 places that might want the thing I was building. The AI summarised the list in seven words, three of them in brackets.

![a 352-row outreach list (send not recorded)](/blog-media/what-my-ai-thinks-of-me/card-2.png)

I can tell you that 156 of those rows had an email address. I have excellent records for the import.

![Commits, merges, receipts and plans were counted; a real outside user doing a real thing was not.](/blog-media/what-my-ai-thinks-of-me/card-3.png)

This is a measurement problem, and I am on the wrong side of it. A commit gets an ID the moment I make it. Importing a contact creates a row even if I never write to the person. To measure a conversation you need a different event, like a reply, and somebody other than me has to produce it.

![Since 2026-06-27 the building system took more effort than any product.](/blog-media/what-my-ai-thinks-of-me/card-4.png)

The file gives a date, June 27, and says that since then the building system took more effort than any product.

![On 11-12 the focus list changed three times in one night.](/blog-media/what-my-ai-thinks-of-me/card-5.png)

Quite a focused evening.

It also searched my ChatGPT messages from December 2022 to October 2026. Five of 9,252 matched its customer-contact keywords, and two of those said I meant to contact an actual person. It admits its search missed at least one customer-facing post I drafted in 2024, so five is an undercount. My best objection to the methodology is another draft.

Then it wrote the sentence.

![3. You are fast with people you know. You rarely approach strangers who might buy.](/blog-media/what-my-ai-thinks-of-me/card-6.png)

It rated its confidence in that one.

![Confidence: low.](/blog-media/what-my-ai-thinks-of-me/card-7.png)

And, to be fair to itself, it said how it might be wrong.

![Could be wrong if: you talk to buyers by phone and never mention it to an AI.](/blog-media/what-my-ai-thinks-of-me/card-8.png)

Two lines up, the same file says I prefer calls. So it left me a way out, and I am not taking it.

![all nine lines, each with my ruling stamped on it](/blog-media/what-my-ai-thinks-of-me/verdicts.gif)

So that is what the AI thinks of me, and it is damn right. I can build. I can't sell. Its phrase for why: "building and tooling were always available in place of the ask." Its fix is to put the one ask only I can make in the first slot of the week. That one is filed as pending my confirmation, which is a polite way to describe me.

One more finding, from the same file.

![one LinkedIn share ever](/blog-media/what-my-ai-thinks-of-me/card-9.png)

I am fixing that one first.
