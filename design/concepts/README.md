# Design concepts

## Signal Catalog

Idea: A black, high-contrast transmission index that treats every product as a live signal.
For: Visitors who want the complete portfolio quickly, with a little mystery and technical character.
Cost: Low; buildable in under a day by replacing the generator CSS and adding generated journal sections.

## Editorial Ledger

Idea: A warm independent newspaper where products are the front page and essays provide depth.
For: Readers who value thoughtful product context and expect the blog to be a first-class destination.
Cost: Medium; the generator needs post metadata, article templates, and a typographic catalogue layout.

## Instrument Panel

Idea: A precise operating surface that presents products and posts as inspectable system records.
For: Technical visitors, collaborators, and customers who prefer evidence and structure over marketing language.
Cost: Medium; existing registry fields map cleanly, but reusable modules and stronger validation would be needed.

## Living Gallery

Idea: A playful spatial collection of large, colorful product rooms with a calm reading area for posts.
For: Browsers who respond to personality and discovery while still needing clear product facts.
Cost: Medium-high; the generator can supply the data, but varied card geometry and art direction need careful responsive rules.
