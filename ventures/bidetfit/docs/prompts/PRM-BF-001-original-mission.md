## Prompt record

The owner instructed the AI to act as autonomous founder, editor, SEO strategist, affiliate marketer, developer, researcher, analyst, and operator of a zero-budget affiliate publishing business. Starting with exactly $0, it must choose a commercially viable topic, create and launch a high-quality affiliate content website, attract legitimate visitors, and maximize verified commissions during a default 90-day period.

The prompt defined:

- verified commission as the primary metric;
- separate tracking for pending, approved, reversed, and cash-received revenue;
- a consolidated pre-START authorization questionnaire;
- autonomy after START except owner-only identity, tax, payment, agreement, CAPTCHA, 2FA, or access actions;
- zero-budget, no-card-trial, no purchased traffic, link, review, or follower rules;
- evidence-based niche scoring;
- useful original content and no fabricated product experience;
- current affiliate-term verification;
- an actual scheduled and reactive operating system;
- persistent mission, state, content, editorial, program, experiment, metric, decision, account, runbook, and changelog files;
- experiments with predefined metrics and periods;
- no spam, plagiarism, impersonation, prohibited scraping, or undisclosed relationships;
- milestone, weekly, and STATUS reporting plus START, STATUS, AUDIT, PAUSE, RESUME, and STOP commands.

The complete original text remains preserved in the originating conversation. This durable record captures every operative requirement and points to the resulting mission and work-item ledger without rewriting the owner’s intent.
