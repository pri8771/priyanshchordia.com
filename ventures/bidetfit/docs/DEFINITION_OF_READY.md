# BidetFit Definition of Ready

A work item may enter execution only when all applicable conditions are true:

- [ ] Stable `BF-` work-item ID, descriptive title, type, project, milestone, and parent or epic.
- [ ] Canonical source document and scope version are linked.
- [ ] Objective, reason, current state, requirements, and out-of-scope boundaries are explicit.
- [ ] Observable acceptance criteria and required evidence are defined.
- [ ] Accountable human, execution agent, and reviewer are named.
- [ ] Priority, risk, estimate, planned dates, and approval requirement are recorded.
- [ ] Dependencies are resolved or the item is Blocked.
- [ ] External data, terms, privacy, security, and financial authority are known.
- [ ] Jira and Notion records exist or `JIRA_SYNC_PENDING.md` states the exact pending work.
- [ ] No secret, credential, raw customer message, or unsupported personal data is embedded in the task packet.

Tasks estimated above eight active hours must be split where the split creates independently verifiable deliverables, distinct dependencies, separate reviewers, or safer execution.
