## Competitive evidence and adjustment
- Bidets: Smart Toilet Fit App Store listing — https://apps.apple.com/pl/app/bidets-smart-toilet-fit/id6797139706
- BestBidets practical buying guides — https://bestbidets.com/
- BidetsUSA recommendation quiz — https://bidetsusa.com/pages/how-to-choose-the-right-bidet-and-toilet

The smart-toilet app validates that exact fit math and source-linked unknowns are valuable, but it also removes any claim that the concept is empty. BestBidets validates commercial search demand while making broad “best bidet” coverage less differentiated. BidetFit therefore narrows its moat to retrofit seats and attachments, special toilet geometry, accessible plumbing, and exact toilet-model by bidet-model records.
