BidetFit/Round vs. elongated
Size problem

“Looks elongated” is not a measurement.

A compact elongated bowl can be shorter than expected, and brands do not all use the same cutoff. Measure the porcelain from the bolt centerline.

The broad ranges

A current BidetKing electronic-seat guide describes round bowls as commonly 16.5–17.5 inches and elongated bowls as greater than 18 inches from the bolt-hole centerline to the front edge. Its attachment guide uses a similar idea, with a slightly broader 16–17.5-inch round range.

A current TUSHY support page uses 18.5 inches as the dividing rule for its electric seats. That does not make one source wrong. It demonstrates that the product's actual dimensions and intended shape control.

Overhang can be functional but awkward

A seat may mount and operate while extending beyond the front of a short bowl. That is not the same as a clean fit. Decide whether the seller considers overhang compatible, whether bumpers contact the porcelain correctly, and whether the return policy allows you to evaluate it without installation.

Shape is only one gate

Correct round or elongated sizing does not solve a French curve, inadequate rear clearance, unusual bolt spacing, inaccessible water connection, or missing outlet.
