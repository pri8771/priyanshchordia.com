How to use the result

A green result means the broad category has no obvious universal blocker. It does not mean every model fits. A yellow result means the category may work but one or more exact-product drawings or installation details control. A red result means at least one broad requirement conflicts with your answers.

Do not buy from this screen alone. Save your measurements, identify the exact toilet model when possible, and compare the product installation drawing and return policy.
