BidetFit/Method
Editorial standard

Compatibility claims need a chain of evidence.

BidetFit exists because “universal” and “fits most” are not enough when a connected hygiene product may become difficult to return.

Evidence hierarchy

  1. Exact manufacturer dimensional drawing and installation manual.
  2. Written manufacturer or authorized-merchant confirmation naming both exact models.
  3. Retailer fit guide with dimensions and explicit limitations.
  4. Public customer reports used only as leads or edge-case warnings, not copied as fact.
  5. BidetFit analysis that combines measurements and labels uncertainty.

What BidetFit does not claim

  • No product has been purchased, installed, handled, or physically tested by BidetFit unless a future page explicitly documents that event.
  • A green checker result is not a guaranteed exact-model fit.
  • Fit guidance is not plumbing, electrical, building-code, accessibility, or medical advice.
  • Merchant claims are not independently certified merely because they are cited.

How corrections work

When a model changes, a source conflicts, or an error is found, the affected page should record an update date, correct the claim, and preserve the reason in the public project changelog. Model-level compatibility entries will eventually include source URL, evidence type, date checked, and confidence.

Commercial independence

Affiliate approval does not determine compatibility. A product without a paying program can still be the correct answer; a high-commission product can still fail the fit screen. Commercial links, when activated, will be labeled and logged.
