BidetFit/How to measure
Ten-minute prep

Measure the toilet, not the old seat.

The mounting holes are the reference point. A seat can overhang, a toilet can be compact elongated, and a model name can hide multiple mold revisions.

Before starting: use a rigid tape measure, remove or lift the current seat if it hides the bolt centers, and record inches to the nearest eighth or hundredth. Do not disconnect plumbing just to measure.

A. Rear clearance

Measure from the centerline of the two seat-mounting holes straight back to the first obstruction: the front of the tank, a porcelain rise, or a French curve. Broad retailer guides commonly cite 1.5 inches for some products and 1.75 inches for many electronic seats, but exact models vary.

B. Mounting-bolt spacing

Measure center to center, not outside edge to outside edge. Broad fit guides show approximately 5–7.5 inches for some attachments and 5.5–7.5 inches for many electronic seats. Some specific products are much narrower; TUSHY, for example, publishes a 5.5-inch maximum for the electric models in its cited support article.

C. Bowl length

Measure from the mounting-hole centerline to the outside front edge of the porcelain. One broad guide describes many round bowls as 16.5–17.5 inches and elongated bowls as greater than 18 inches. A current TUSHY support article uses 18.5 inches as its own round-versus-elongated dividing rule. That disagreement is useful: the exact product rule is more important than a universal label.

Take two photos

  1. A side view showing whether porcelain rises behind the bolt holes.
  2. A plumbing view showing the wall shutoff, supply hose, and tank connection—or proving that those connections are concealed.

Photos do not replace measurements, but they help a merchant or manufacturer support team see French-curve and skirted-toilet problems that numbers alone miss.

Measurement is screening, not approval. Compare your notes with the exact product dimensional drawing and installation manual. A return policy may exclude products once connected to water.
