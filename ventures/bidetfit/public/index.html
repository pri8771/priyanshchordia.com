The fast screen

Four ways a purchase goes wrong

Fit is not just round versus elongated. A product can match the bowl and still fail at the tank, bolts, valve, or outlet.

01

Rear geometry

French curves and rising porcelain can collide with the housing of a seat or attachment.

02

Mounting dimensions

Bolt spacing, bowl length, and the flat area behind the holes must match the exact installation drawing.

03

Plumbing access

Skirted toilets can hide the tank connection where a standard T-adapter expects to go.

04

Power

Electric seats need a suitable receptacle and product-specific cord reach—not a permanent extension-cord workaround.

05

Model variation

Compact elongated and remolded toilet versions can defeat broad model-name claims.

06

Return limitations

Hygiene products can have strict unused-only policies, making pre-purchase verification unusually valuable.
