BidetFit/Disclosure
Commercial transparency

No affiliate links are active in this beta.

BidetFit is designed to earn affiliate commission eventually, but compatibility analysis launches before merchant tracking links.

When links become active

If a merchant approves BidetFit, an eligible purchase made after clicking a clearly labeled affiliate link may generate a commission at no added charge to the buyer. The exact program, rate, attribution window, and approval status are recorded in the project ledger and rechecked before publication.

How links will be labeled

Pages with commercial links will include a clear disclosure before or near the first affiliate link. Buttons will not hide that a destination is a merchant. BidetFit will not cloak, alter, or redirect tracking links in a way that violates program terms.

What money does not change

  • Fit results are based on measurements and evidence, not commission rate.
  • No fake product testing or ownership claims.
  • No paid ranking placement disguised as editorial analysis.
  • No commission is reported as earned until attribution is verified.
  • Pending, approved, reversed, paid, and cash-received amounts remain separate.

Launch status

As of August 25, 2026, affiliate applications submitted: 0; approved programs: 0; active tracking links: 0; verified commission: $0.
