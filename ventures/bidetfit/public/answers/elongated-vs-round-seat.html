Fit question

Will an elongated toilet take a round bidet seat?

Usually no. Manufacturer fit guidance treats round and elongated as different seat geometries. Measure the bowl before buying.

Short answer

Choose a seat shape that matches the toilet bowl. An elongated bowl generally needs an elongated seat; a round bowl generally needs a round seat. BidetFit does not recommend mixing shapes as a workaround; an exact-model compatibility confirmation matters more than whether the bolts line up.

What manufacturers say

  • Bio Bidet compatibility guide — says to match seat and bowl shape and explains why the bowl must support the seat.
  • Bio Bidet “Will a bidet fit my toilet?” — describes measuring from the mounting-hole centerline to the front of the bowl, then checking bolt spacing and tank clearance.

Manufacturer sources checked September 13, 2026. These are general screens, not universal dimensions: use the exact toilet and seat drawings and ask the manufacturer about an unusual or uncertain match.

What to measure

  1. Bowl length: measure from the centerline of the seat mounting holes to the front of the bowl, then compare with the exact seat drawing.
  2. Bolt spacing and rear flat clearance for the seat housing.
  3. Whether the seat overhangs or leaves gaps after mounting.
