The fit checker currently performs its calculations on the page and does not submit the form to a server.

Current data behavior

  • No account or login is required.
  • The checker does not transmit entered toilet measurements to BidetFit.
  • No advertising, affiliate-tracking, email-capture, or analytics script is loaded by BidetFit in the launch beta.
  • The web host and network infrastructure may retain ordinary request logs under their own operational policies.
  • External source links take you to third-party sites with their own privacy practices.

Future analytics

If privacy-respecting traffic measurement is added, this page will be updated before activation and the public source will identify the implementation. BidetFit will not silently reuse Primandir analytics or CRM identifiers.

Future email or affiliate features

An email form or merchant link will receive a clear explanation before data leaves the site. Affiliate links may allow the merchant or network to attribute a purchase according to its own policy.
