Hindsight privacy policy

Hindsight is designed to keep your data on your device.

What is collected Nothing is collected by default. Hindsight has no account system and no advertising or analytics SDKs. Any data you create stays in the app's local storage on your device.

What is shared Nothing is sold, rented, or shared with third parties.

Deleting your data Deleting the app removes all locally stored data. There is no server-side copy to request.

Children Hindsight is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information.

Changes Material changes to this policy will be published on this page.
