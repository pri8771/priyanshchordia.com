PartyTable privacy policy

Draft updated July 29, 2026

The short version

PartyTable is a local pass-and-play party game. Version 1 has no account, developer backend, ads, analytics, tracking, or third-party SDKs. The developer does not receive your game data.

Information stored on your device

PartyTable stores local seat names or labels, game-night history, settings such as haptics and party mode, and whether After Dark mode was enabled. This information is kept in the app's local settings and used only to resume and personalize play.

What the app does not access

PartyTable does not request location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or notification access. It sends no device identifier or gameplay record to a developer server because version 1 has no server.

After Dark mode

After Dark is an optional content setting. Its age confirmation and preference are handled locally on the device. PartyTable does not send the selection to the developer.

Deletion

In-app controls can clear saved night history. Deleting the app removes its active local app data from the device. Apple-managed device backups, if enabled, are governed by Apple and your device settings.

Changes

If PartyTable adds online multiplayer, accounts, purchases, cloud storage, or analytics, this policy and its App Store disclosures will be updated before release.

Contact

Email support@priyanshchordia.com for support or privacy questions.
