AuraFit AI privacy policy

Draft updated July 29, 2026

The short version

AuraFit has no account, developer-operated server, advertising, tracking, or analytics. Outfit photos and scores are processed locally in the app. AuraFit does not upload them to the developer.

Information stored on your device

AuraFit's app storage can contain photos you capture or import, locally generated scores and tips, style matches and color palettes, scorecard images and reveal clips, and app preferences.

Active copies remain in AuraFit's private app storage unless you choose to save or share an output. Apple-managed device backups, if enabled, are controlled by Apple and your device settings.

How photos are analyzed

Analysis uses Apple's Vision and Core Image frameworks plus deterministic on-device heuristics. The current release does not bundle a learned outfit-classifier model. Processing occurs on the device; AuraFit has no developer cloud-processing step or queue that uploads photos for later analysis.

Scores are subjective guidance about an outfit and photograph. They are not a measurement of a person, and AuraFit does not identify or recognize people.

Permissions

  • Camera — to capture a photo you choose to scan. The camera is not used in the background.
  • Import from Photos — uses Apple's system photo picker. AuraFit receives only the image you select and does not request broad Photo Library read access.
  • Photo Library (add) — when you save a scorecard or reveal clip, including through the optional auto-save setting.

You can change camera and photo-add permissions in iOS Settings. Declining camera access still allows photo import through the system photo picker.

Sharing

AuraFit opens Apple's share sheet or photo-library save flow only after you choose Share, Save, or an enabled auto-save action. A recipient or selected service controls its copy after you share it.

Purchases and Apple services

AuraFit Pro subscriptions and one-time template unlocks are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles payment information and purchase records; while the app is running, AuraFit reads verified entitlement state to unlock features. StoreKit, Manage Subscription, and Apple's standard EULA use or open Apple services governed by Apple's policies.

What the developer does not receive

AuraFit does not send the developer your photos, scores, app preferences, name, email address, phone number, advertising identifier, or cross-app activity. It contains no advertising, attribution, or developer analytics SDK.

Deletion

Delete All Fits removes saved scans and their associated images from active app storage. Deleting AuraFit removes its remaining active local app data. Files you saved or shared, Apple purchase records, and Apple-managed device backups are outside AuraFit's control.

Changes

This policy and AuraFit's App Store privacy disclosures will be updated before any changed data handling is released.
