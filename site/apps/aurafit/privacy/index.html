AuraFit privacy policy

Draft updated July 29, 2026

The short version

AuraFit does not collect your data. There is no account, no server, and no analytics. Every photo you scan is analyzed on your iPhone and stored only on your iPhone. The app has no networking code of its own, so there is nowhere for your photos or scores to go.

Information stored on your device

Photos you capture or import for a scan, the scores, tips, style match, and color palette the app produces, any scorecard images and reveal clips you generate, and your preferences all live in AuraFit's private app sandbox on your device. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

How photos are analyzed

Analysis runs entirely on your device: Apple's Vision framework estimates body pose and person segmentation, Core Image measures image and color signals, and AuraFit uses a deterministic color-based heuristic for the closest style match. Nothing is uploaded for processing. Scores are subjective guidance about an outfit and a photograph; the app does not identify, recognize, or profile anyone.

Permissions

Camera access is used only to capture the photo you are about to scan. Importing uses Apple's system photo picker, so AuraFit receives only the image you select. Photo Library add access is requested only when you save scorecards or reveal clips. You can revoke any of these in iOS Settings.

Purchases

AuraFit Pro subscriptions and template unlocks are processed by Apple through StoreKit. Apple handles the payment; AuraFit never sees your payment details and keeps no purchase records of its own beyond what StoreKit provides on device.

Sharing and deletion

Scorecards and reveal clips leave the device only when you tap Share or Save and choose a destination in the iOS share sheet. Settings > Delete All Fits removes every saved scan and its images immediately, and deleting the app removes everything else.

Changes

If the app's data practices ever change, this policy and the in-app policy screen will be updated before the change ships.
