Roam

Your travels, coloured in.

A private map of the places you have explored. Switch tracking on and Roam fills in supported U.S. Census ZIP Code Areas as you move. Anywhere else in the world, add countries and cities by hand.

In TestFlight beta — not on the App Store yet. No Roam account, developer cloud, advertising, or third-party analytics. Apple services process what MapKit, Core Location, and StoreKit require.
