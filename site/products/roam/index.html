Privacy

Your experience remains yours.

No Roam account, developer cloud, advertising, or third-party analytics. Apple services process what MapKit, Core Location, and StoreKit require.

A clear boundary

ZIP Code Areas are Census approximations, not postal delivery boundaries. Automatic tracking requires location permission.
