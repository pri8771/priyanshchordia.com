Svara

Practice. Learn. Return.

A private daily companion for spiritual practice and cultural reconnection: short guided practices, an ordered learning path called Aaroh, and the stories behind what you already do.

In TestFlight beta — not on the App Store yet. No account, developer analytics, advertising, or cross-app tracking. Practice data and reflections are not sent to a developer-operated server.

How it works

Four steps you will repeat.

  1. 01

    Choose a practice, lesson, festival, or story.

  2. 02

    Move through the experience with bundled audio where it exists.

  3. 03

    Return through optional local reminders and continue your path.
