Anjali

A sacred pause, not a session.

One short prayer for the moment you are in — with the Devanagari, a transliteration, and a plain-English meaning on the same screen. Then you close the app.

In TestFlight beta — not on the App Store yet. No account, backend, analytics, advertising, tracking, third-party SDKs, or purchases. Preferences and activity remain on-device.

How it works

Three steps, then put it down.

  1. 01

    Choose the moment you are in.

  2. 02

    Read or chant one short prayer at your own pace.

  3. 03

    Save what matters or close the app when the pause is complete.
