Mala

A quiet digital mala.

Keep your place. Feel each count. Finish without looking. One tap advances one bead — and the count survives a phone call, a locked screen, or the rest of the day.

In TestFlight beta — not on the App Store yet. No account, advertising, analytics, tracking, or developer-operated service. Practice information stays in the app's local storage.

The app

Five screens. That is the whole app.

Why it exists

Three things a physical mala already does well.

01

Count by feel

A large practice surface and restrained feedback keep attention on the repetition.

02

Resume naturally

An interrupted round can continue without turning practice into a productivity task.

03

Make it yours

Private labels, multiple mala styles, and local history support a personal ritual.

How it works

Four steps, then you can put the screen away.

  1. 01

    Choose a round and an optional private label.

  2. 02

    Tap through each repetition at your own pace.

  3. 03

    Complete the round and keep the history on your device.
