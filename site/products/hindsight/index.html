Hindsight

Make the decision. Remember the reasoning.

A private journal for learning how you decide. Write down what you believed and how sure you were — then come back when reality has answered, and grade the outcome and the process separately.

In TestFlight beta — not on the App Store yet. Hindsight runs on-device with no networking code, backend, account, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

The app

Capture, wait, review.

Why it exists

Hindsight is only useful if it is honest.

01

Record the belief

Capture an expectation and confidence before knowing the result.

02

Seal the entry

Keep the original thinking intact until it is time to revisit it.

03

Reflect on evidence

Compare belief and outcome, then add context that a simple result cannot hold.

How it works

Four steps, spread over months.

  1. 01

    Write what you expect and how confident you feel.

  2. 02

    Seal the prediction until its review date.

  3. 03

    Record the outcome and reflect on what changed.

Privacy and trust

You will only be honest in it if nobody else can read it.

Hindsight runs on-device with no networking code, backend, account, analytics, advertising, or tracking.
