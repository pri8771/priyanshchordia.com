AuraFit

An outfit check that stays on your phone.

Capture or import a full-body photo and get a Fit Score for outfit, colour, pose, lighting, framing, and background — computed entirely on your iPhone. The photo never leaves the device.

In TestFlight beta — not on the App Store yet. AuraFit analyzes photos entirely on device with no account, backend, analytics, or tracking. The app has no networking code of its own, and every feature is free.
