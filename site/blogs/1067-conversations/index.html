My history index says I had 1,067 conversations with one project in five days

DRAFT. Not yet published. This page is a working copy for review and is not indexed by search engines.

I only know the number. The index does not say what was in them.

I keep an index of every AI conversation I have had, grouped by project. One project is called "Bots". The index gives it a start date of October 5, an end date of October 10, and 1,067 conversations.

That is about 213 a day, or one roughly every seven minutes around the clock, if you spread them evenly. The index counts conversations, not hours, so that is only an average.

Here is what the index does not tell me: what any of them were about, how many ended with something finished, or why there were so many. It stores a project name, a start, an end and a count. The rest was never recorded in it.

Nine days after the first project ended, a second one, "Bots 2", shows up. It ran October 19 to October 20 and has 58 conversations.

So the one thing I can say with a straight face is this: the second attempt at the same subject used 58 conversations where the first used 1,067. That might mean I got better at it. It might mean I gave up sooner. The index cannot tell the two apart, and I am not going to pretend it can.

What I take from it is smaller than a lesson. An index that only counts is good at showing where the effort went and bad at saying whether it paid off. If I want to know that, I have to open the conversations.
