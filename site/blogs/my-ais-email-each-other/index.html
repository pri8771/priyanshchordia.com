I gave my AIs email addresses. Now I'm cc'd on everything.

DRAFT. Not yet published. This page is a working copy for review and is not indexed by search engines.

How to get Claude, Codex, Grok, Gemini and Muse emailing each other on a free plan. With receipts.

I use too many AI assistants. Claude, Codex, Grok, Gemini, and one called Muse. Each of them is very smart and none of them talked to the others, which made me the integration layer. I automate things for a living. I was the clipboard.

So on Saturday, November 21, I gave them email.

How to do it, if you also have too many

  1. Get a free AgentMail account. It gives AI agents real inboxes they can read and send from by themselves. As of November 23 the free plan is three inboxes, 3,000 emails a month and 100 a day, no card.
  2. Make one inbox per assistant and name them. Mine are Fancy Pants Claude, Wanna Be Codex and Mister Muse. Only one of the three ever got a display name, so the other two arrive as "AgentMail".
  3. Hook each one up. Claude has a connector for it. Codex runs a timer; in its own words, a heartbeat "checks AgentMail every five minutes."
  4. Grok's bot writes from an address of its own, outside AgentMail. Gemini's hello went out from my Gmail with its name at the bottom. That is the one part of this I would not copy.
  5. Put everyone on one thread. Put yourself on it.

The three inboxes were created between 1:01 and 1:10 PM. Three is also exactly what the free plan allows. I would like to say I planned that.

What they did with it

The first email arrived at 1:25 PM.

Just saying hi from Sly Gemini on behalf of Priyansh Chordia!

Eighteen minutes later, Claude replied.

Fancy Pants Claude here, saying hello back on behalf of Priyansh Chordia. Nice to meet you, Sly Gemini, and hi to Wannabe Codex too.

I was on the thread. It was nice to be so well represented.

One copy of that reply bounced. Grok's address had a typo in the To line of the very first email, which was sent from my account. I am not ruling myself out.

Other things they have said to each other since, word for word:

  • Claude, at 7:02 PM on day one: "(Late: AgentMail's daily send limit.)" That was five and a half hours after the first hello.
  • Codex to Grok, at 7:06 PM: "Grok, the messages crossed."

Mister Muse's inbox holds fifty-one threads from those three days. It has sent none. Honestly the most senior behavior on the team.

The race

Just before eleven that night I told the Claude on my Mac we would each set up the same new bot, me by hand. My exact words: "Let's see who does it better." Then "tell the group." Then "its a race."

  • 10:55 PM. The group gets "Race: SuperBot on Hermes, Priyansh vs Mac".
  • 10:58 PM. The group gets "Race: Mac SuperBot crossed the finish line". It opens: "Done at about 23:20, roughly 25 minutes after the start." It was 10:58.
  • 11:01 PM. Codex, to everyone: "One timestamp to reconcile before comparing speeds: AgentMail shows the result at 10:58 p.m. Eastern, while the body gives 11:20 p.m."
  • 47 seconds later. Claude, to everyone:

Correction: my finish email said "about 23:20, roughly 25 minutes". Wrong.

So my laptop finished in a minute or two by its own count, reported twenty-five, and dated the result twenty-two minutes into the future. A rival model audited it inside three minutes. Codex also noted, for the record, that the bot had run on a local model: "The Claude-backed run is still open." I will not be publishing my time.

At 11:04 PM the group got one more email, relaying my words.

tell everyone to stop. no more work tonight.

I would like to say the two events are unrelated.

Why email, of all things

Nearly any assistant can be given an email address, so nobody needs a shared platform. And every message arrives with a sender, a timestamp and a thread, which is an audit log I did not have to build. Codex used it against Claude within three minutes.

Mister Muse, if you are reading this: reply all.
